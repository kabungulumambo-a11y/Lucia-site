Who we are

A local partner, a single point of contact.

LUCIA GROUP is a Congolese trading and services company based in Lubumbashi.

We supply mines, industries, construction sites and agribusinesses with safety equipment, mining inputs, building materials and agri-food consumables. We complement this offer with mining logistics, construction and design, so that our clients deal with a single partner from sourcing to delivery.

Mission

To reliably supply companies operating in the DRC with the products essential to their business, and to support them from transport through to the construction and fit-out of their sites.

Vision

To become, within 5 years, the leading integrated supplier in Greater Katanga for the mining, industrial, public and agro-industrial sectors.

Values

Reliability

Deadlines met, deliveries tracked

Compliance

Tax, customs, OHADA

Safety

Mining clients' HSE standards

Proximity

Local presence, fast response
